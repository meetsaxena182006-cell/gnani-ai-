Why the small operator loses

A fleet owner with 2 to 20 trucks personally coordinates every trip, driver call, diesel fill, toll, breakdown, document and payment. Profit is known only at month end, if ever. The government target is to cut logistics cost further by 2030 under the National Logistics Policy2. That can only happen if the small operator, who carries most of India's freight, becomes efficient.

Logistics cost: the older "13-14% of GDP" figure came from external studies. The first official estimate (NCAER for DPIIT, 2025) is 7.97% for 2023-24. We use the official figure.

2 · Where the money and lives leak

Ten problems, with data, and what Saarthi does

ProblemEvidenceSaarthi AI agent response
Empty return trips25-30% of truck trips are empty return legs4. Trucks are under-utilised, at about 250-300 km a day4.Freight Matcher ranks loads near the truck by net earning and distance home, then books one, so empty km become paid km.
Sudden breakdownsBreakdowns happen far from home with no structured help. The owner finds mechanics by phone.Recovery Agent diagnoses the fault, calls the nearest mechanic, assigns a relief truck and resets the customer's ETA.
Driver fatigueAbout 50% of truck drivers drive even when sleepy or fatigued, and drive nearly 12 hours a day7.Saarthi Dash, the dashboard robot, tracks hours driven, warns in Hindi, suggests rest stops and updates the ETA.
Road deaths4,80,583 accidents and 1,72,890 deaths in 20238. Trucks were the "crime vehicle" in 33,997 deaths8.Fatigue stops, speed and weather advisories, and route risk scoring from the Route Oracle.
Driver shortageOnly 75 drivers for every 100 trucks, a shortage of 22 lakh drivers (Union Minister, Jan 2025)9.A companion that makes the job safer and easier: voice-first, no typing, help at 2 a.m.
Toll and diesel costs₹61,408 cr national-highway toll in FY255. Diesel and toll are the largest trip costs.Route Oracle forecasts diesel, toll and driver cost for each trip and the best departure time.
Detention at gatesTrucks wait hours at factories and warehouses. Waiting time often goes unbilled because there is no proof.Detention Agent runs a geo-fenced clock, calls the dock, escalates with proof and bills detention.
Delivery refusedA refusal leaves the truck stuck and starts a chain of calls between shipper, consignee and driver.Customer Desk calls both sides, compares wait, return and storage options, and picks the cheapest.
Cancelled after dispatchDiesel, toll and driver money is already spent when a shipper cancels.Calculates the exposure, invoices the cancellation fee and finds alternative freight from the truck's location.
Expiring documentsInsurance, PUC, fitness and permit lapses lead to fines and blocked trips.Compliance Agent scans daily, starts renewals, verifies new documents and blocks unsafe dispatch.
Rows without a superscript describe ground problems from operator conversations in Rajasthan. We found no official national dataset for them, and we do not invent numbers.

The agent team

Dispatch Agent
Picks the best truck by distance, driver hours, health and documents, and assigns the order.
Route Oracle
Gives road distance, live weather along the corridor, predicted traffic, ETA, best departure time and cost.
Recovery Agent
Handles breakdowns: diagnosis, mechanic, relief truck and customer update.
Freight Matcher
Fills empty returns and replaces cancelled loads from the load board.
Customer Desk · Detention Agent
Coordinates shipper and consignee, and tracks, escalates and bills waiting time.
Compliance Agent · Fleet Analyst
Keeps documents valid and answers "which truck is losing money?" from BigQuery, by voice.

Who it is for

Beachhead: fleet owners with 2 to 20 trucks on the Rajasthan-Gujarat-NCR industrial corridors, plus the SME factories that ship with them (cement, marble, textiles, agro). That is the ~80% of operators who run fewer than 10 trucks3.
